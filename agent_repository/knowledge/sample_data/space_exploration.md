# A Brief History of Space Exploration

Space exploration began with the launch of the Soviet Union's Sputnik 1 satellite in 1957.
This event marked the beginning of the Space Race between the USSR and the USA.

## Key Milestones

- **1957:** Sputnik 1, the first artificial satellite, is launched.
- **1961:** Yuri Gagarin becomes the first human to orbit the Earth.
- **1969:** Apollo 11 lands the first humans on the Moon.
- **1998:** The International Space Station (ISS) construction begins.
- **2012:** The Curiosity rover lands on Mars to search for signs of past life.

Modern space exploration is driven by both government agencies like NASA and private companies such as SpaceX and Blue Origin. Their goals include returning to the Moon, sending humans to Mars, and studying the wider universe.
